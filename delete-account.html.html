Delete Your Truck Traffic Account

You may request deletion of your Truck Traffic account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Deletion

Email us at: privacy@trucktraffic.in

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Please include your registered phone number in the email.

Data Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Posted loads
  • Account data associated with your phone number

Data Retention

Some records may be retained where required by law or for fraud prevention purposes.

Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.
